📺 Install on Android TV

Android TV doesn't support direct browser downloads. Use one of these methods:

1 Easiest: Install Downloader app from the Play Store on your TV, then enter the APK URL manually.
2 ADB: Enable Developer Options on TV, then run adb install <apk-file> from your computer.
3 Scan QR: Scan with your phone to download the APK, transfer to TV via USB or network share.
